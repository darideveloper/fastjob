Classification of Cookies

1. According to their origin:

  • First-party cookies: Those sent to the User's device from a device or domain managed by the Controller and from which the requested service is provided.
  • Third-party cookies: Installed by a third-party company, aimed at obtaining useful data to improve our Website. Examples include: number of visits, origin, search keywords, or peak traffic hours. You can block these via browser settings.

2. According to their purpose (AEPD Classification):

  • Technical cookies: Those allowing navigation through a website, platform, or application and the use of different options or services, including editor tools to manage page operation and enable services (e.g., controlling traffic, identifying the session, making purchases, processing payments, fraud control, etc.). Exempt from Article 22.2 LSSI obligations when used to provide the requested service.
  • Personalization cookies: Retain information to allow the user to access the service with specific characteristics that differentiate their experience (language, number of results, region, etc.). Exempt from LSSI obligations if chosen by the user.
  • Measurement or analysis cookies: Allow the Controller to track and analyze user behavior on websites, including quantifying ad impacts, to introduce improvements.
  • Behavioral advertising cookies: Store user behavior data obtained through continuous observation of browsing habits to develop a specific profile to show targeted ads.

3. According to their persistence:

  • Session cookies: Collect and store data while the user accesses a web page, used only to maintain information for the requested service on a single occasion, disappearing when the session ends.
  • Persistent cookies: Remain stored on the device and can be accessed and processed for a period defined by the controller (from minutes to years).
