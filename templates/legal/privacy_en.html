3. Use of Google & Microsoft APIs, Data Transfer, and Sharing

FastJob uses the gmail.send (Google API) and Mail.Send (Microsoft Graph API) permissions to send emails on your behalf.

Google and Microsoft Data Sharing and Disclosure:

No sharing or transfer: FastJob does not share, sell, rent, trade, transfer, or disclose your Google or Microsoft user data (including OAuth authentication tokens, email address, or profile information) to any third parties or external applications under any circumstances.

Exclusive use: This data is stored securely and is used solely and exclusively to automate sending your emails (resumes/CVs) from your own account to the destination addresses you select. No data is transferred to advertising networks, data brokers, or advertising intermediaries.

FastJob's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Service Data and Integrated Platforms

To ensure the correct functioning of our platform, we process and store the following specific data:

  • Identity Data (OAuth): We collect the name, email address, and profile picture obtained through Google or Microsoft OAuth to create and manage your user account.
  • CV Content and Secure Storage (DigitalOcean): Resume PDF files uploaded to our platform are stored privately and securely on DigitalOcean Spaces servers. For distribution to companies, we generate unique download links (UUIDs) that have a limited validity time (5 minutes) to prevent unauthorized public access to your personal documents.
  • Sending Logs: We record sending activity, recipient companies, timestamps, and sending status to provide the service and manage your credit consumption.
  • Billing Data (Stripe): We process payments through Stripe. We only store Stripe customer identifiers and transaction status; we do not store any credit card or debit card data on our own servers.

5. Opt-out System and Global Blacklist

We respect the right of recipients not to receive unwanted emails. When a company uses the "Unsubscribe" link included in the automated emails, they are added to a global blacklist. This means no FastJob user will be able to send emails to that specific address again, ensuring compliance with the privacy preferences of the recipients across the entire platform.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Upon deleting your account, we immediately delete your resume files and your Google or Microsoft OAuth access tokens. However, for legal and tax compliance reasons, we will retain records of financial transactions and Stripe invoices for the period required by competent tax regulations.

7. Contact for GDPR Rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability of your personal data by sending an email directly to our Data Protection Officer (DPO) at dpo@basquekide.es.
